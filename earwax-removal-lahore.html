About Ear Cleaning

Ear cleaning is a safe and quick way to remove wax, dirt, and debris from the ears. Best ENT specialist in Lahore often recommends professional ear cleaning when home remedies don’t help or when earwax blocks hearing.

This simple procedure helps prevent infections and hearing problems. It is done by ENT specialists using special tools. Never try to clean deep inside the ear on your own. Professional ear cleaning is painless and keeps your ears healthy. The cost depends on the clinic and the method used.

Ear Cleaning Benefits

Here are the benefits of getting your ears cleaned by a doctor:

  • Removes built-up wax and debris

  • Improves hearing and ear comfort

  • Prevents infections and irritation

  • Reduces the risk of blockage and dizziness

  • Safe and fast when done by a specialist

  • Clears ringing or fullness in the ears

Ear Cleaning Side Effects

While safe, ear cleaning can have minor side effects in some cases:

  • Mild discomfort during the process

  • Temporary dizziness

  • Slight irritation inside the ear

  • Rare chance of infection if not done properly

  • Ear canal dryness or itching after cleaning

Most of these effects are short-term and go away quickly.

Ear Cleaning Procedure

Here’s what happens during a professional ear cleaning:

  1. Checkup:

The doctor uses an otoscope to check your ears.

  1. Preparation:

You’ll sit comfortably while the doctor explains the steps.

  1. Cleaning:

Wax is removed with suction, a curette, or warm water flush.

  1. Drying:

The ear is gently dried. Drops may be given to prevent irritation.

  1. Aftercare:

You may be told how to care for your ears at home.

Ear Cleaning Price in Lahore

Ear cleaning in Lahore usually costs between PKR 500 to PKR 3,000. The price depends on:

  • City and clinic

  • ENT specialist’s fee

  • Cleaning method used

  • Equipment and follow-up care

It’s best to ask the clinic for exact charges before the visit.
